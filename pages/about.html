About Me

Storytelling is the process through which we share what it is to be a human being.

I am an Australian-born film / commercial director based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. I moved here after living in Los Angeles for seven years where I studied and made theater and films, wrote and edited screenplays, and auditioned as an actor. I have a fundamental belief that all people are doing the best they can, and that means that through good storytelling, there’s real hope for a better world. I like to work with groups of ambitious, creative people, and I try to create environments where people deliver their best work.
