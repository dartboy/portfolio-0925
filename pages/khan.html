Khan Academy

I wrote and directed this commercial spot to highlight Khan Academy’s mission of personalized, self-paced learning. Our focus was on creating a clear, cinematic story that shows how education can meet students where they are. From scripting and directing to working with actors and crew, I managed the full creative process, and thanks to the images our DP Jack Ohrman made, we created a polished piece that feels like it could live on air today.
